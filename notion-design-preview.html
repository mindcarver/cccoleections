Design Philosophy

❌ Typical Design
  • Bold accent colors everywhere
  • Large shadows and gradients
  • Excessive whitespace
  • Decorative animations
  • Marketing-focused layout
✅ Notion Approach
  • Minimal accent color usage
  • Barely visible shadows
  • Efficient use of space
  • Fast 0.15s transitions
  • Content-first layout

Key Characteristics

🎯 Content First

Information takes absolute precedence over decoration. Every pixel serves a purpose.

📖 System Fonts

Fast-loading native fonts feel familiar and reduce cognitive load.

🌫️ Minimal Depth

Shadows are barely noticeable (0.04 opacity). Borders create separation.

⚡ Snappy Feel

Fast 0.15s transitions provide instant feedback without feeling sluggish.
