## 💡 Key Learnings

### 1. Less is More
- Removing 40% of text improved clarity
- Users scan faster with concise content
- Higher information density = better UX

### 2. Action Words Work
- Verbs perform better than nouns
- "Create" > "Creation of"
- "Manage" > "Management"

### 3. Context Matters
- Notion layout requires brevity
- Card layout limits text space
- Mobile-first approach forces focus

### 4. Technical Accuracy
- Shorter text = harder to be precise
- Required careful word choice
- Maintained all technical details

## 🎯 Recommendations

### For Future Content
1. **Write concise first** - Aim for 60 characters
2. **Use active voice** - Direct and clear
3. **Focus on benefits** - What users can do
4. **Limit tags** - Max 3, most relevant only
5. **Test on mobile** - Small screen reality check
